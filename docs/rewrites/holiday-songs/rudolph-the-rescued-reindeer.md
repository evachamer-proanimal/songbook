# Rudolph The Rescued Reindeer

*John Eric Sakars*

Rudolph, the rescued reindeer  
Had a very difficult life  
And if you ever saw him,  
You would say he faced great strife  
All of the other reindeer  
They faced many hardships too  
Living in a speciesist society  
Is not an easy thing to do  

Then one foggy Christmas eve,  
Activists came to say,  
"Using animals is not right.  
You don't have to pull a sleigh tonight."  
Then all the reindeer escaped  
They went to a sanctuary  
Rudolph the rescued reindeer  
Now you're happy, safe, and free

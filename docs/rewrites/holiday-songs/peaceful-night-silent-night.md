# Peaceful Night (Silent Night)

*Jason Oliver*

Peaceful Night  
Vegan Night  
All is calm  
All is bright  
Animals living  
Safe and happy  
Finally freed from captivity  
Rescued by heavenly grace  
Living compassionately  

Peaceful Night  
Vegan Night  
Human beings  
Now believe  
Animals are friends  
Not property  
Protecting them  
Is our noble duty  
Liberated from hatred and greed  
Living in heavenly peace

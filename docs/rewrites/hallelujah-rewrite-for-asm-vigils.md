# Hallelujah Rewrite for ASM Vigils

*Music: Leonard Cohen · Lyrics: Sarah Hewson*

We see your face, we hear your cry  
The hope is we can unify  
We won’t give up, you will not be forgotten  
We’ll tell your story, project your voice  
Gain freedom for all and we’ll rejoice  
But, wish you could feel that Hallelujah  
Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelu-u-u-u-jah  

Our offer here is not enough  
Weaving through the screams, a voice of love  
A tiny flame in the wind and darkness  
On the scent of despair, a dusting of hope  
That one day we finally throw you a rope  
Sanctuary leads to Hallelujah  
Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelu-u-u-u-jah  

We recognize your life and worth  
Your strength and beauty upon your birth  
You will not walk to death alone and silenced  
Liberation is coming, we’ll open their eyes  
And show them how justice will be our prize  
Our lives are one, we all need hallelujah  
Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelu-u-u-u-jah  

I can still feel the heat as you arrived  
In blood and waste you were confined  
You still reached out to greet me face-to-face  
Vibrations of peace were all I could give  
A promise to remember that you had lived  
A soothing melody of hallelujah  
Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelu-u-u-u-jah  

We’re haunted by the pain of your mother  
Solemn acceptance in your brother  
Sneezing, coughing, crying just like us  
We watched you try to break the chain  
Many are watching, feeling that pain  
Peace and change are coming, hallelujah  
Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelu-u-u-u-jah  

We’re bringing love to Hell on Earth  
Our early compassion had a rebirth  
We’ll find this here inside of every human  
We’ll apply the lessons of history  
The cages will open, we’ll all be free  
Our movement is truth, our goal is possible  
Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelujah, Hallelu-u-u-u-jah

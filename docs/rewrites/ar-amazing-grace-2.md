# AR Amazing Grace

AR Singer  

Amazing grace how sweet the sound  

That saved a wretch  

like me.  

I once was lost  

but now I'm found.  

Was blind but now I see.  

'Twas love that taught  

my heart to feel  

And love that brought me here  

How precious did  

that love appear  

The hour I first believed.  

We’ve hurt them for ten thousand years  

Its time to set them free  

Release their chains and let them live  

Open your eyes and see  

In pens and cages  

Locked up tight  

their pain and cries  

Ignored  

Suff'ring every day and every night  

Then led to the killing floor  

No grass, no love, no sun, no sky  

Just unenduring pain  

Until it’s time for them to die  

And it’s done again and again  

Innocent and helpless  

They are born  

To live a life so cruel  

From their mothers love  

They are all torn  

Nevermore to feel secure.  

Across the globe  

We hear their call  

To them our oath we give  

We'll march. Speak out, and  

Sing to all  

Until they let animals live.  

Amazing grace must spread the love  

And reach each human being  

Touch their hearts and their souls  

For evermore  

And bring peace to all living things  

Amazing grace, how sweet the sound  

That saved a wretch like me.  

I once was lost,  

but now I'm found  

Was blind, but now I see

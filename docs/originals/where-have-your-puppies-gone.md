# Where Have Your Puppies Gone?

*Ora Goldman*

- [Recording (voice and piano)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zLrD9HSyZXhZgjDpmnc_YCwnUkL_6wU5/view?usp=sharing)
- [Photos and video clips of Anna](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1-DuyLwoFIqAGGq_M2-NXFmpHeF3p0c-M?usp=sharing)

Anna, Oh Anna, where have your puppies gone?  
Caged by the thousands. Sold for their biology.  

(Repeat)  
Anna, Oh Anna, where have your puppies gone?  
Spinning and howling, cut open and bleeding.  

Anna, Oh Anna, what happened to you?  
Kind people rescued you, hearts so true!  
And you romped and you played and you stepped on grass.  
Eating and sleeping and shining with Love.  

But Anna, oh Anna, where have your puppies gone?  
Still trapped inside. We must free them ALL!  

[Repeat ad lib]

# I Will Wait for You

*Mis Kyrene*

Could someone please  
explain something to me  
There are fearful eyes as far as mine can see  
I’m trying to find my mom’s  
In the dark of these four walls,  
is she trying to find me too?  
Why can I not hear her calls?  

Would she even recognize my face?  
I’ve grown a bit since I was brought here  
to this place  
I’ve been asking all around  
kicking and screaming on the ground  
How can no one know a thing inside this space?  

CHORUS  
In the stillness of my own despair  
a heartbeat of hope comes through somewhere  
I know that help is somewhere real close by  
They say that you are not allowed  
but I feel the strength of a loving crowd  
when your light spills through the cracks inside  
So I will wait another day  
cause at least I know you tried  

While it’s true that I’m not trapped inside a cage  
my life is hardly free from pain or rage  
There’s nowhere for me to turn  
The heat makes my eyes burn  
I’ve cried out so much, my throat feels very dry  
I’m much too young for me to want to die  

CHORUS  

BRIDGE  
My captors sent the light away  
It’s harder now to face another day  
if all this stays the same  
Survival’s just a game  
that I don’t know if I can ever win  
I’m told it doesn’t matter if I do  
that after this it only gets much worse  
Please tell me that’s not true,  
please save me from this curse  
I know you’re coming back for me  
so I will wait for you  

In the stillness of my own despair  
a heartbeat of hope pounds through somewhere  
I know that help came in the nick of time  
You marched past all the laws and lies  
towards the sound of desperate cries  
and this time, no one could stop you now  
cause compassion’s not a crime  
no, compassion’s not a crime

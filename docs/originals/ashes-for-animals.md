=== "Lyrics"

    In the coughing crowds of cages, under lights that won’t turn off  
    Straining eyes from bright and violence  
    Craving sleep, just simple peace  
    You were thin by the glow of my headlamp  
    Little bugs crawled on your skin  
    No place to hide or to run to  
    As us strangers filed in  

    After months and days of living angry hands grabbed you like air  
    Did you fight against the fated, did you still care?  
    An orphan who birthed seven orphans

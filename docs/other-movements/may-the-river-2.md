# May the River

- [Recording](https://soundcloud.com/theresaannmartin/may-this-body-be-a-bridge)

May this body be a bridge  
For the healing of this land  
May the river flow through us  
Cleansing greed from our hands  

We are we are born from the water  
We are, we are, made from the land  
Teach us, teach us, oh great mother  
to bring to bring peace to the land
